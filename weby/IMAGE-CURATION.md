# Image Curation — Caviar (`thedesigns.org/weby`)

**Art direction:** High-end editorial studio. Near-black, cool silver / platinum / pearl. Fashion/art-house, not a generic agency.  
**Palette:** `#070708` bases · pearl white `#f4f5f7` · silver `#c5cad3` · platinum highlights. **No gold, champagne, or warm yellow.** No neon.  
**Mood:** Quiet luxury, grain, negative space. Caviar as pearl-on-black, not clip-art eggs.

---

## Global rules

| Do | Don't |
|----|--------|
| Low-key, cool grade, optional film grain | Gold, amber, tungsten wash, champagne highlights |
| Real photography: product, interior, food, team, venue | Invented client logos, fake testimonials, neon VR |
| Negative space for headline overlay | Busy center-weighted clutter |
| 16:9 hero; 4:3 or 3:4 for editorial tiles | Watermarks, clip-art, wedding shots used as Caviar case studies |

**Export:** WebP/AVIF, 1920×1080 hero, 1200×800 tiles, quality ~80–85. Cool shadows, silver rim light only.
